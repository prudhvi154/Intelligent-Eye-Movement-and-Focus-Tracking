Calibration Wizard

This process maps your eyes' movement and head posture to coordinate points on your physical display. This ensures accurate off-screen gaze flagging during your test.

Instructions:
  1. Sit upright at normal reading distance from your camera.
  2. A blue target circle will appear at 5 specific spots on the screen.
